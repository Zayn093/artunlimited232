Questions

Good to know

Why do some pieces say "on request"?

Resin, mosaic and string art are made in different sizes and designs. Tell us what you like and we will send the exact price before you confirm.

How much is delivery?

It depends on your city and the size of the order. We tell you the exact charge on WhatsApp before anything is sent.

Can a piece be customised?

Yes. Names, colours, sizes and designs can be adjusted on most pieces. Mention it in your order note or message us.

Will my piece look exactly like the photo?

Very close. Each piece is made by hand, so small differences in colour and detail are natural and make it one of a kind.
